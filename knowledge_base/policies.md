# Internal Policies

Compensation review happens annually each March. Any salary adjustment above 8 percent
requires sign-off from both the team lead and the head of the relevant function.

Customer data may not be sent to external email addresses. Internal reports are written to
the shared workspace and circulated through internal distribution only.

Quarterly reporting convention: a quarter is labelled by calendar year and quarter, for
example 2025Q1. Revenue is recognised at the point of shipment, not at order placement.
