# Northwind Robotics - Company Overview

Northwind Robotics builds industrial manipulators and perception hardware for warehouse
automation. The company was founded in 2016 and is organised into four teams: Robotics
(manipulator hardware and control), Perception (vision and sensing), Platform (fleet software
and controllers), and Sales.

The product line has four SKUs. NR-100 "Arm 100" is the entry manipulator and the volume
seller. NR-220 "Arm 220 HD" is the heavy-duty manipulator, sold in low volume at a high unit
price. NR-310 "Vision Pod" is the perception module and ships in the highest unit counts.
NR-450 "Fleet Controller" is the platform product that coordinates a deployed fleet.

Northwind sells into three regions: NA (North America), EU (Europe), and APAC. NA is the
largest region by revenue. APAC is the newest and currently buys mostly perception hardware
rather than full manipulator systems.
